Education, Wellness & Training

1. Educational Support Services

Educational support services provide academic guidance, tutoring, and learning resources to help students improve their performance. They focus on strengthening core concepts and supporting continuous learning through structured mentoring and assistance.

2. Skill Development & Learning Assistance

Skill development services enhance practical, technical, and professional abilities through training programs and workshops. They help individuals build confidence, improve employability, and become job-ready for various career opportunities.

3. Nutrition Club Activities

Nutrition club activities promote awareness of healthy eating habits and balanced diets among students and communities. They encourage wellness-focused practices through interactive sessions, guidance, and lifestyle improvement initiatives.

4. Yoga Activities

Yoga activities support physical fitness, flexibility, and mental relaxation through guided practice sessions. They help reduce stress, improve concentration, and promote overall health and well-being.

5. Cultural Activities

Cultural activities encourage participation in arts, traditions, and community events that promote creativity and expression. They help build teamwork, cultural understanding, and appreciation of diversity.
